The plan

Where the sets go

Hard sets per week, from week 1 to week 3. Back and side delts get the most, because they make the width of the V.

10+ hard sets per muscle per week grows muscle;1 going above 20 hasn't clearly beaten 12–20.2

The belly goes last

Six weeks of daily ab training didn't shrink belly fat or waist size.9 A 2022 meta-analysis found the same for training any one spot.10 The waist comes in through the calorie deficit. Ab work stays for core strength.

    Four weeks, four gears

      RIR = reps in reserve, how many more clean reps you could do. Going to true failure every set hasn't beaten stopping 1–2 reps short.3

        Convenience store picks

        • 即食鸡胸肉 ready-to-eat chicken breast: about 19–24 g protein per 100 g on pack labels.19 Choose plain or low-sodium.
        • 茶叶蛋 tea eggs from the counter pot.
        • 纯牛奶 milk, 无糖豆浆 unsweetened soy milk, high-protein yogurt (高蛋白酸奶).
        • 关东煮: eggs and tofu, not the fish balls and sausages.

        Read the back of the pack. The 蛋白质 line is protein per 100 g; 能量 is energy in kJ (÷ 4.18 = kcal). Rule of thumb: 10 g+ protein per 100 kcal is a good protein food. (Example label, not a real product.)

        Supplements, optional

        • Creatine monohydrate, 3–5 g a day, any time. The best-supported supplement for strength and training gains, and safe at this dose.1112
        • Whey only to close a protein gap. Food first.

        Cut these

        • Milk tea, juice, cola.
        • The oily sauce at the bottom of 红烧 and 干锅 dishes.
        • Late-night 外卖 and 烧烤.

        Canteen prices: a Nanjing community canteen charges ¥15–22 for one meat and three vegetables.20 No reliable university canteen figures found [gap].
